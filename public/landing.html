ZITADELLE

Two lines of advance. One village at the end of both.

A top-down multiplayer tank battle set on the southern face of the Kursk salient. Germany attacks village by village toward Prokhorovka, the Soviet Union holds the line, and every tank either side sends in comes out of a reserve pool that won't refill.

01 / Combat

How a battle is fought

There are no levels or upgrades inside a battle. Every vehicle has fixed historical stats, and you win by what you take and what you can afford to lose.

01

Take the line

The attackers fight forward from A5 and B5, one village at a time. If the defenders retake a starting village, that line is defeated and stays locked for the rest of the battle.

  • Hold more strength in the zone to take it
  • Prokhorovka is larger and takes twice as long
  • Light tanks count double in a zone
02

Spend reserves

Each side has a fixed pool of light, medium and heavy tanks and tank destroyers. A side that has used up its reserves and has nothing left on the field loses.

  • 5 vehicle categories
  • Remaining reserves shown to both sides
  • A new battle starts 30 s after one ends
03

Carry the load

Every deployment adds to your personal logistics load, which drains at one second per second. A Tiger II takes most of your ten minutes. A light tank costs nothing.

  • 10:00 logistics limit
  • Bots follow the same rule
  • Your load clears when a new battle starts
04

Break them apart

Armour is modelled by facing and thickness. When a shell gets through, it damages whatever is behind the plate: tracks, engine, fuel or the ammo rack.

  • Hull-relative tank controls
  • Neutral, clutch and wheeled steering
  • Houses and the church stop shells
02 / Armour

What's behind the plate

Every vehicle has four hidden components. Where a shell goes in decides what it hits, so you aim for a component, not just for the tank.

Tracks

Outside the armour. A side hit damages them even if it doesn't penetrate. Without tracks the vehicle can't drive or turn its hull.

10 s repair
Engine

Top speed drops to 30% and the hull turns at half rate. It pours white smoke that everyone can see until it's repaired.

20 s repair
Fuel tank

Destroying one costs the vehicle an extra 12% of its health at once, and that side burns for four seconds.

15 s repair
Ammo rack

Destroying it costs 15% of the vehicle's health, and the ammunition cooks off in sparks and bangs.

20 s repair

05 / FAQ

Questions

Can I play Zitadelle now?

Yes. It runs in your browser. Enter a commander name, pick a side and deploy. You don't need an account or a download.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Bots fill out both sides and follow the same reserve and logistics rules as players, so a battle is never empty.

What do I need?

A desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse. WS drive, AD steer the hull, the mouse aims the turret, and right-click fires your machine guns.

Is it historically accurate?

The villages, lines and vehicles come from the southern face of Kursk in July 1943, and armour, guns and handling follow the real vehicles. Some later vehicles are included for balance and variety.
